Public Disaster Preparedness & Safety

Citizen earthquake survival instructions and community response guidelines.

🚪

Drop, Cover & Hold On

During shaking, drop to your hands and knees. Cover your head and neck under a sturdy table or desk. Hold on until shaking stops.

🎒

Emergency Go-Bag Kit

Keep 72 hours of drinking water, non-perishable food, flashlight, battery radio, first aid supplies, and emergency whistle accessible.

🔊

If Trapped Under Rubble

Cover your mouth with cloth to avoid dust. Tap loudly on pipes or wall beams in rhythmic sets of 3 so RES-Q-NET acoustic sensors locate you.
